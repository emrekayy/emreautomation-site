Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Emre Automation is a personal automation application that uses Google OAuth and the YouTube Data API to upload and manage videos on a YouTube account explicitly authorized by the user.

Google User Data

The application accesses only the Google and YouTube permissions required to perform the actions authorized by the user.

How Data Is Used

Google user data is used solely to authenticate the authorized YouTube account and perform requested YouTube operations such as uploading videos.

Data Storage

OAuth credentials may be stored securely by the automation system so that scheduled YouTube operations can continue without requiring the user to sign in each time.

Data Sharing

Google user data is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Revoking Access

Users can revoke the application's access at any time through their Google Account security and third-party access settings.

Data Deletion

Disconnecting the Google account or deleting the application's stored credentials removes its ability to access the YouTube account.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer using the support email associated with this application.
